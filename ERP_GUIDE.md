# MAA PADMAWATI SAREES - ERP System Guide

## System Overview

A complete retail and wholesale billing system designed for saree business with customer management, inventory tracking, and professional invoicing.

---

## Main Features

### 1. Retail Quick Sale (🛒)
**Perfect for: Individual customers, walk-in sales**

- **Fast checkout** - Complete billing in under 30 seconds
- **Customer management** - Add new customers on the fly
- **Barcode scanning** - Scan product IDs for instant lookup
- **Real-time totals** - See amounts update as items are added

#### How to use:
1. Go to **Retail Sale** tab
2. Select or add a customer
3. Enter bill number and date
4. Scan/enter product ID
5. Click "Add Item" to invoice
6. Click "Save & Print Invoice"

---

### 2. Party/Bulk Sale (📦)
**Perfect for: Wholesale orders, distributor sales, bulk purchases**

- **Detailed invoicing** - All fields for professional billing
- **Tax management** - CGST, SGST, IGST support
- **Transport tracking** - LR details, shipping info
- **Amount controls** - Discount and additional amount fields

#### How to use:
1. Go to **Party/Bulk Sale** tab
2. Select a party/supplier from dropdown
3. Fill invoice details (bill no, date)
4. Enter gross amount with discounts
5. System auto-calculates net amount with taxes
6. Add transport details if needed
7. Click "Create Party Sale Bill"

---

## Customer Management

### Add Customer in Retail Sale
- Customers are managed within the Retail Quick Sale tab
- Click "Add New Customer" when no customer is selected
- Enter: Name, Phone, Email (optional), Address

### Bulk Add Parties
- Use the main Reports or Settings section to manage all parties
- Each party can be marked as "Customer", "Supplier", or other types

### Customer Data Used on Bills
- **Name** - Appears on invoice header
- **Phone** - Contact reference
- **Address** - Delivery/billing address
- **Email** - For digital invoices (future)

---

## Product & Inventory

### Product Items (Individual Tracking)
Each dress/product gets a unique ID system:
- **Format**: PROD-20250121-A7F2K (auto-generated)
- **QR Code**: Printed label attached to dress
- **Pricing**: Individual purchase + sale price per item
- **Status**: Available, Sold, Damaged, Reserved

### Lookup Products in Sales
1. Go to **Products** tab to generate new item IDs
2. Print QR codes and attach labels to dresses
3. In sales, scan the QR or type the ID
4. All details auto-populate instantly

---

## Billing & Invoicing

### Invoice Components
Every bill includes:
- **Header**: Company name, date, bill number
- **Customer Details**: Name, phone, address
- **Item List**: Each product with quantity and rate
- **Calculations**: Subtotal, GST (18% default), Total
- **Footer**: Terms, conditions, signature lines

### Types of Invoices
1. **Retail Invoice** - For individual customers
2. **Party Invoice** - For wholesale/bulk orders
3. **Tax Handling** - CGST/SGST for intra-state, IGST for inter-state

## Best Practices

### For Fast Checkout
- Pre-label all sarees with unique IDs and QR codes
- Train staff to use barcode scanner
- Keep customer list updated
- Use numeric shortcuts for frequent parties

### For Accuracy
- Always verify customer before billing
- Double-check quantities before adding items
- Review totals before printing
- Keep printed bills organized by date

### For Inventory
- Update product item status immediately after sale
- Mark damaged items right away
- Reconcile inventory weekly
- Track stock levels in Products section

## Troubleshooting

### Customer Not Found
- Check if customer is created in system
- Search by phone number instead of name
- Create new customer if first time purchase

### Product Not Found
- Verify product ID format (PROD-YYYYMMDD-XXXXX)
- Check if ID is scanned correctly
- Generate new ID if needed from Products tab

### Invoice Not Saving
- Ensure all required fields are filled (marked with *)
- Check internet connection
- Try again or contact support
